# Bundled material

- Geist: SIL Open Font License, from the Google Fonts distribution. License: `assets/fonts/Geist-OFL.txt`.
- JetBrains Mono Regular: SIL Open Font License. License: `assets/fonts/JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt`. Runtime ASCII ramps are calibrated against this exact bundled font.
- GlyphicaDots: original eight-dot geometry generated by `tool/build_dot_font.py`; MIT licensed as part of this project. It covers U+2800–U+28FF with a fixed 600-unit advance and 1200-unit line. Third-party apps use their own fonts when pasted.
- Philosopher source: supplied by the user in the Stitch design reference folder. Bundled as an offline sample, with no external image URLs or runtime requests. Its original rights remain with its owner.
- Test portrait (`test/fixtures/portrait.png`): NASA photograph of Eileen Collins, public domain, distributed as scikit-image `astronaut.png`.
- Test pet (`pet.png`): Chelsea, photograph by Stefan van der Walt, CC0.
- Outdoor test scene (`landscape.jpg`): SpaceX rocket launch scene, public domain, distributed as scikit-image `rocket.jpg`.
- `dark.png` and `format.webp` are derived locally from the public-domain portrait. Logo, line, and transparency fixtures are original programmatic shapes.

Fixture provenance and licenses are documented in the [official scikit-image dataset documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.25.x/api/skimage.data.html). The three source photographs were downloaded from the matching [v0.25.2 source distribution](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/tree/v0.25.2/skimage/data). Test fixtures and contact sheets are development material and are **not** bundled in the app.

Flutter dependencies retain their own licenses, available through Flutter's license registry. No analytics or network client is used by application code. Some picker/share dependencies have transitive platform utilities; their existence is not an image-processing network path.
